==================================================
PRODUCT OVERVIEW
==================================================

Product name:
OrderPilot

Description:
OrderPilot is an AI-powered operations dashboard for delivery-first restaurants.

The product helps restaurants:
- manage delivery operations
- monitor live orders
- reduce operational chaos
- track kitchen load
- identify delays
- analyze performance
- centralize operational visibility

This is NOT a delivery marketplace.

This is operational SaaS.
