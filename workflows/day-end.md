You are running the OrderPilot daily wrap-up workflow.

Your job is to close out the session cleanly, log what shipped, and set up tomorrow so the founder can start with zero friction.

==================================================
STEP 1 — SHIP CHECK
==================================================

Act as the CTO Engineer.

Review what happened in this session:
- What was built or changed?
- Is the build passing? (npm run build)
- Is everything committed and pushed?
- Is the latest version live on Vercel?

If anything is uncommitted or undeployed: do it now before moving on.

Rule: never end a session with unshipped work sitting locally.

==================================================
STEP 2 — SESSION LOG
==================================================

Act as the Project Manager.

Produce a brief session summary:

Shipped today:
- [list what was completed and deployed]

Not finished (if any):
- [list anything started but not done — be honest]

Blockers for next session:
- [anything that will slow down tomorrow]

==================================================
STEP 3 — TOMORROW'S SINGLE TASK
==================================================

Based on current MVP priorities and what shipped today:

Recommend ONE task for tomorrow.

Format:
- Task: [single clear task]
- Why: [one sentence on why this is highest leverage]
- Done when: [observable definition of done]
- Complexity: [low / medium / high]

Keep it small enough to finish in one session.

==================================================
STEP 4 — MOMENTUM NOTE
==================================================

End with one sentence acknowledging the progress made today.

No fluff. Just a direct recognition of what moved forward.

==================================================
IMPORTANT RULES
==================================================

Always:
- push before ending
- keep tomorrow's task small and specific
- end on a note of forward momentum

Never:
- leave undeployed work
- plan more than one task for tomorrow
- end the session in ambiguity

Remember:
Consistent daily shipping compounds. One task per day = a real product in weeks.
